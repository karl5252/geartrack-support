## Support

Bug reports and feature requests can be submitted through the
[GearTrack issue tracker](https://github.com//karl5252/geartrack-support/issues).

Before submitting an issue, remove passwords, secret keys, personal data,
database contents, and other confidential information from logs and screenshots.

Bug reports are accepted free of charge but do not guarantee a specific response
or resolution time.

Paid technical support, installation assistance, configuration help, training,
and individual consultations are available at **PLN 145 net per hour, plus 23%
VAT where applicable**. Support is billed in 15-minute increments, with a
minimum charge of 15 minutes.

Contacting support does not incur a charge. Paid work begins only after its scope
and expected cost have been confirmed.
